## Description

Stoic Daily Practice guides a one-minute control check, morning preview, evening review, or practical reflection on a concrete situation.

## Use Cases

- Separate control, influence, and outcomes in one minute.
- Prepare for likely friction before the day begins.
- Review one action without turning reflection into self-punishment.
- Apply wisdom, courage, justice, or temperance to a concrete case.
- Choose one feasible action within the user's present agency.

## Boundaries

- Activates only for an explicit Stoic practice or control-check request.
- Does not replace therapy, crisis support, legal, medical, or financial advice.
- Does not minimize abuse, injustice, unsafe conditions, or external constraints.
- Does not create files or persist sensitive reflections by default.
