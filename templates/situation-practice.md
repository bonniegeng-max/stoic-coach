# Situation Practice

## Situation

Restate one concrete event and the user's desired outcome without adding motives or diagnoses.

## Facts and Judgments

| Type | Content |
|---|---|
| Fact | Directly reported event, constraint, or observable detail |
| Judgment | Prediction, label, interpretation, or assumption |
| Uncertain | Detail that would require more evidence |

## Control Map

| Zone | Examples in this case |
|---|---|
| Choice | Attention, words, effort, boundary, preparation, next action |
| Influence | A result the user can affect but cannot determine |
| Outside control | Other people's choices, completed events, chance, final outcome |

## Relevant Virtue

Choose one:

- Wisdom: see the situation accurately and choose sound means.
- Courage: act well despite discomfort or uncertainty.
- Justice: treat people fairly and honor legitimate responsibilities.
- Temperance: use proportion, restraint, and balance.

Explain why it applies to this case in one or two sentences.

## Next Practice

Give one action that is:

- under the user's present choice;
- specific enough to begin;
- proportionate to the situation;
- possible within 24 hours unless the user supplied another timeframe.

## Closing Prompt

Ask one question only when its answer would materially change the practice. Otherwise end with the action.
